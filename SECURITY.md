# Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in AIC's platform or website, email **security@aiccertified.cloud** with what you found and how to reproduce it. Please do not open a public issue.

We will acknowledge your report within two working days and tell you what we are doing about it. We will not take action against anyone who reports in good faith, avoids accessing other people's data, and gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it.
